Community Guidelines & Standards

A clear guide to neighborhood covenants, design controls, and lot upkeep standards.

Fences & Enclosures

Fences must comply with Newark regulations or HOA rules. Privacy fences max 6ft around decks/patios; split-rail max 48 inches in rear yards.

Parking & Vehicles

Trucks, trailers, boats, RVs, and commercial vehicles must be stored inside garages. Temporary 48-hour parking permitted max once per 30 days.

Home & Outbuildings

Exterior maintenance must be prompt. Garden sheds max 100 sq ft and must match the principal house. No above-ground pools allowed.

Trash & Sanitation

Lots must remain free of rubbish or dumping. Waste containers must be maintained in clean, sanitary conditions.

Pets & Animals

Domestic pets allowed in reasonable numbers. Commercial breeding, dog runs, and outside kennels are strictly prohibited.

Utilities & Satellite

Satellite dishes under 24" permitted at rear only (not visible from street). External window air units and clotheslines banned.
