Community Guidelines Quick Reference

Key summaries of Willow Bend rules and standards for quick homeowner access.

All fences built within Willow Bend require prior written ARC approval before construction begins.

  • Approved Materials: High-grade wood (cedar/treated), wrought iron, or approved white/tan vinyl. Chain-link fences are strictly prohibited.
  • Maximum Height: 6 feet in rear yards; perimeter privacy fencing must follow standardized community setbacks.
  • Placement: Fences may not extend forward of the rear corners of the home structure.

To preserve neighborhood appearance and emergency access across Willow Bend, specific vehicle guidelines are enforced:

  • Street Parking: Overnight street parking is prohibited. Vehicles must be parked in garages or paved driveways.
  • Commercial Vehicles & RVs: RVs, boats, trailers, and commercial trucks may not be parked on driveways for longer than 48 hours for loading/unloading. Storage must be off-site or enclosed in approved garages.
  • Maintenance: Major auto repairs are not permitted on driveways or open lots.

Trash and recycling bins must be kept out of public view except during designated collection times.

  • Set Out Times: Containers may be placed at curb no earlier than 6:00 PM on the evening prior to scheduled pickup.
  • Storage: Cans must be stored out of sight inside garages or behind an approved screened structure/fence on non-pickup days.

Exterior alterations or secondary structures must complement existing residence design:

  • Sheds: Must match home paint, trim, and roofing materials. Pre-approval from ARC required.
  • Paint Palette: Repainting with new exterior colors requires submittal of swatches for approval.
