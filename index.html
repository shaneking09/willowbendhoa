Quick Reference

Community Rules & Guidelines

Key rules enforced within the Willow Bend Community. Filter or search topics below for immediate answers.

Fence Standards

All perimeter fencing requires prior ARC written approval. Fences must not exceed 6 feet in height and must be constructed of approved shadowbox wood or vinyl materials. Chain link is strictly prohibited.

ARC Approval Required

Vehicles & Parking

Overnight street parking is prohibited to maintain emergency access. Commercial vehicles, RVs, boats, and trailers must be parked inside closed garages or stored offsite.

No Overnight Street Parking

Pet Guidelines

Household pets are limited to customary domesticated animals. All dogs must be kept on a physical leash when on common areas. Owners must immediately clean up after their pets.

Leash & Waste Compliance

Trash & Recycling Cans

Trash containers may be placed curb-side no earlier than 6:00 PM the evening prior to scheduled collection. Cans must be stored out of sight inside garages or behind fencing on non-pickup days.

Curbside Pickup Timing

Sheds & Storage Structures

Detached sheds must match the primary home's exterior shingles and paint colors. Sheds must conform to rear setback requirements and receive prior ARC approval.

ARC Approval Required

Lawn & Property Maintenance

Turfgrass must be regularly mowed and kept under 5 inches in height. Weeds must be managed, and landscaping beds kept free of debris to maintain high community aesthetics.

Regular Maintenance
Architectural Review

Planning Exterior Improvements?

To protect property values, any modifications to the exterior of your home or lot—such as installing a fence, shed, patio, roof replacement, or painting—require prior approval from the Architectural Review Committee (ARC).

Submit plans, plot maps, and contractor specs directly via TownSq.
Track review status and receive formal electronic decision letters online.
Average review timeframe is under 14 business days.
