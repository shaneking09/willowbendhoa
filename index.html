Willow Home Owners Association, Inc.

Serving the Willow Bend Community

The Willow Home Owners Association, Inc. is a non-profit corporation established to preserve the architectural integrity, safety, and property values of the Willow Bend Community.

All lot owners in Willow Bend are automatic members of the Association. Working in partnership with Associa Community Management, the Board oversees common area reserves, enforce recorded Deed Restrictions, and manages community finances.

1

2

3
Attach Site Plans

Upload plot maps, contractor specs, materials, and color swatches, then submit for review.

Covenants Quick Search

Rules & Regulations Summary

Search key restrictions recorded for the Willow Bend Subdivision.

Fences & Enclosures

  • Privacy Fences: Restricted to rear decks/patios, maximum 6 feet high.
  • Yard Fences: Rear yard fences limited to split-rail style up to 48 inches.
  • Pool Fences: Must be ornamental; solid pool fences are prohibited.
  • Requires prior ARC approval via TownSq before installation.

Sheds & Outbuildings

  • Garden sheds are strictly limited to 100 square feet maximum.
  • Shed siding and roofing materials must match the main house.
  • Must be positioned in the rear yard behind the main home line.

Vehicles & Parking

  • RVs, boats, trailers, commercial, or inoperative vehicles cannot be parked outside.
  • Must be stored inside an enclosed garage out of view.
  • Temporary loading/unloading allowed up to 48 hours max once every 30 days.

Trash & Property Upkeep

  • Lots must be kept clean and free of rubbish, brush, or waste dumping.
  • All waste containers must be maintained in clean, sanitary condition.
  • Lawns must be regularly mowed and edged.

Swimming Pools

  • Above-ground swimming pools are strictly prohibited.
  • In-ground swimming pools are permitted with prior ARC approval.
  • Hot tubs and saunas are permitted subject to screening.

Pets & Animals

  • Domestic household pets are permitted in reasonable numbers.
  • Commercial breeding, dog runs, and outdoor kennels are prohibited.
  • Pets must not be allowed to run loose or create a noise nuisance.
